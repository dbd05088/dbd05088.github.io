Minhyuk Seo

Computer Science Ph.D. Student · UC Berkeley EECS

I'm a Computer Science Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS) at UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Joseph E. Gonzalez .

Before joining UC Berkeley, I was a Research Associate at KU Leuven , working with Prof. Tinne Tuytelaars . I received my M.S. and B.S. degrees in Computer Science from Yonsei University , advised by Prof. Jonghyun Choi .

My research focuses on human-like AI, inspired by how humans learn and evolve. In particular, I focus on (i) continual learning and (ii) collaborative learning, with the goal of enabling human-like learning that continually acquires knowledge over time and evolves through interaction with others.
